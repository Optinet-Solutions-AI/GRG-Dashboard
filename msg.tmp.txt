Rankings: stop presenting an old position as last week's

"تجميد حساب التداول" read "↓ Lost was 31" in the week of 2026-10-13 for Saudi
Arabia. It had not been ranked #31 the week before: Saudi was missing from the
2026-10-02 export entirely, so the comparison stepped over that week and landed
on 2026-09-21, three weeks back. The cell announced a drop that did not happen
in the week it was shown under.

The previous position comes from lag() over the rows that exist, which is last
week's only when that keyword/market was actually checked last week. A market
the checker never returned leaves no row at all, and nothing downstream could
tell the difference — an absent row looks the same as a pair never tracked.

ranking_grid_multi now returns prev_week alongside prev_position, and the page
compares it with the week above on the page. Where they differ the cell drops
the arrow and the red flag — both describe a move between two weeks, and no
move can be claimed across a gap — and shows the position dated instead:
"was 31 · 21 Sep". The number is worth keeping; the implied recency was not.

A market that WAS checked and simply did not rank is untouched: its previous
position is null, so it reads "Not in top 100" with no flag, as before. A real
week-over-week loss still shows "↓ Lost was 4" in red.

The function is dropped and recreated rather than replaced — a set-returning
function's return type cannot change in place.
